# Oxygen Generator Simulation

A MATLAB-based numerical model of a pressure-swing adsorption oxygen generator, developed as part of my Mechanical Engineering BEng research at the University of Warwick.

## Project Overview

The model simulates oxygen purity, nitrogen breakthrough, temperature variation and pressure drop across a zeolite-packed adsorption bed.

## Methods

- One-dimensional transient modelling
- Method of lines and spatial discretisation
- Stiff ODE solving
- Adsorption kinetics and equilibrium modelling
- Sensitivity and numerical-stability analysis

## Key Results

- Predicted 72.2% outlet oxygen purity at 18 seconds
- Identified 10% nitrogen breakthrough at 6.87 seconds
- Predicted a temperature rise from approximately 298 K to 306 K
- Calculated approximately 0.01 bar pressure loss across the bed

## Tools

MATLAB and Simulink
